Secure, private bot backups

A small, limited-access application that helps preserve Flower Market Telegram bot database backups in Google Drive.

Limited-access utility

What this application does

Flower Market Backups uses a user-authorized connection to Google Drive to upload, list, and restore encrypted backup files produced by a privately managed Telegram marketplace bot. It is not a public backup service or a consumer-facing Telegram bot.

  • Scheduled backups: an independent server task creates database snapshots for disaster recovery.
  • Encrypted storage: backup contents and filenames are intended to be encrypted before uploading to Google Drive.
  • Defined destination: the backup process targets a designated, authorized Google Drive folder.
  • Portability: stored files can be used to restore the bot on another server.
Current rollout: local server backups are active. Google Drive integration is being configured and will only become active after account authorization and testing.

Google Drive access

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0. The Google account holder explicitly authorizes access and can revoke it at any time in their Google Account connections.

Depending on the permissions granted during authorization, Google's consent screen may authorize broader Google Drive access than the single destination folder. Our intended backup workflow operates only on the designated folder and its own backup files.

For details on data handling, read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Who can use it?

This utility is restricted to its operator and specifically authorized collaborators. It does not offer public registrations, paid plans, advertising, or analytics.

Contact the operator via the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
