Conditions for the limited-access Flower Market Backups integration.

Effective date: October 17, 2026

1. Purpose

Flower Market Backups is a privately operated tool intended to transfer and restore encrypted operational backups for a Flower Market Telegram bot. It is not offered as a public commercial service.

2. Authorized use

Only the operator and explicitly authorized collaborators may connect a Google account. Authorization must be provided by the Google account holder or a person permitted to act on their behalf. Users must not attempt to access unrelated data.

3. Google Drive permissions

Google OAuth grants the permissions displayed during consent. The operator intends to use access solely for the designated backup folder and associated backup files. The account holder may revoke the authorization at any time through their Google Account settings.

4. Backup limitations

Backups support recovery but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability or perfect recovery in every case. The operator is responsible for securely retaining recovery keys, monitoring backup jobs, periodically testing restoration, and maintaining backups independently of the primary server.

5. Changes and discontinuation

The integration may be modified or discontinued by the operator. In such cases, stored data remains subject to the Google Drive account holder's access and deletion controls.

6. Contact

Use the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen to contact the operator.
